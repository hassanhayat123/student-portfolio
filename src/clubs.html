Club Categories

Our clubs are grouped into a few broad categories. Pick the one that sounds like you, or try a couple. Most students are part of more than one club.

Technology

Technology Clubs

For students who like building things. Members work on coding projects, take part in hackathons and run hands-on sessions on web development, AI and robotics.

e.g. Computing Society, Robotics Club, Google Developer Student Club

Sports

Sports Clubs

Cricket, football, basketball, table tennis and more. Sports clubs hold regular practice sessions and represent the university in inter-university tournaments.

e.g. Cricket Club, Football Club, Badminton Society

Culture

Cultural Clubs

Music, drama, art and photography. Cultural clubs organise the big campus shows, festivals and exhibitions, and they always need performers and backstage help.

e.g. Dramatics Club, Music Society, Photography Club

Debate

Debate Societies

Practice public speaking, argue both sides and compete in national debating and MUN competitions. Great for building confidence, even if you are shy at the start.

e.g. Debating Society, Model United Nations, Literary Circle

Not sure which one to pick?

Come to the Club Fair at the start of each semester. Every club sets up a stall, so you can talk to members and ask questions before you sign up. You can also check the upcoming events and just show up to one.
