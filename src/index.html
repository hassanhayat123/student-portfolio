Why join a club?

University is more than lectures and exams. Clubs are where most students make their closest friends, pick up skills that never show up on a transcript and find out what they actually enjoy doing.

  • Meet new people outside your own department and batch.
  • Build real skills like teamwork, public speaking and event management.
  • Grow your CV with leadership roles, competitions and projects.
  • Take a break from studies and do something you love.
