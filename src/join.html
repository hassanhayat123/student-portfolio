How to Join a Club

Joining a club takes less than a week from start to finish. Follow these steps and you will be at your first meeting in no time.

  1. Pick a club

    Have a look at the club categories and shortlist one or two clubs you like. You can also talk to members at the Club Fair in the first week of the semester.

  2. Register online

    Fill in the club registration form on the student portal using your university email. You will need your roll number, department and semester.

  3. Attend the orientation

    Every club holds a short orientation for new members. You will meet the executive team, hear about plans for the semester and get added to the club group.

  4. Start taking part

    Come to meetings, sign up for events and volunteer when you can. The more you show up, the more you get out of it.
